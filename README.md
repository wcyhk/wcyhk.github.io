## 世界的盡頭 → 世界的復甦 / The World Reborn

The About section contains a local Three.js 3D world. Drag to orbit, use the wheel to zoom, and awaken the Education, Technology and Creativity beacons. The monumental black-hole gate remains dark through the first two lights; the third transforms its event horizon into a radiant sun and warms the surrounding world. Arrow keys and +/- also control the camera; Space pauses the star flow and R restores the view. On phones, enable exploration explicitly, then choose “完成探索” to return to page scrolling. “重新探索” resets all three beacons and restores the black hole.

`time-experience.js` and its stylesheet retain their existing entry filenames; `world-model.mjs` owns discovery state, `world-scene.mjs` builds the procedural landscape, and `world-post.mjs` supplies the HDR glow. Three.js r170 and OrbitControls are vendored with their MIT license. No external image, model, API, account or audio is needed for this work. Rendering stops offscreen, respects reduced motion, caps resolution and offers lightweight rendering. Personal introduction text remains in HTML and can be read even if WebGL is unavailable.

The landscape uses procedural PBR stone and engraved inlays, environment reflections, soft shadows and beveled ruins. The black hole has an animated accretion disk and lensed halo; the sun adds granulation, sunspots and magnetic prominences. Fine and wide bloom retain highlight detail. `world-materials.mjs` owns generated textures and environment resources. The sky emerges behind the final project cards; the former white wave (`assets/world-shore.svg`) is retained but no longer referenced. Gallery footer padding is 72px on desktop and 40px on phones; profile top padding is 32px / 20px, keeping the three sections close together. Empty search results retain a readable light background. Transparent top and bottom masks on the scene merge into the same navy background; `assets/world-stardust.svg` carries faint stars into the profile, then fades before the detailed biography. Decorative layers ignore pointer input. No additional animation or JavaScript is used for these transitions.

The last visible gallery row uses a translucent navy glass surface, light text and a gold primary action. CSS row selectors follow the three-column desktop grid and two-column grid below 951px, including search and category filtering. Card links and poster images retain their original destinations and artwork.

Beacon beam fade clamps its UV-derived fractional-power base to [0,1]. Multisampled edge interpolation can otherwise feed a negative value into the power calculation; the resulting invalid alpha is amplified by HDR bloom into black tiles at high camera angles. Keep this clamp when changing the beam shader.

`world-rebirth.mjs` coordinates the original sculpted ancient enemies, cloud palaces and sanctuary. The 26-second awakening camera uses quintic easing, flies near at 3.2 seconds, holds an identical eye-level pose until 11 seconds, then gradually pulls back to a half-overhead view. Pointer or keyboard orbit takes over; reduced motion skips the flight. A shared holyRadius expands from the sun at (0,3.9,-3.2) after 1.4 seconds, at five world units per second. `world-spells.mjs` gives opaque skin, fangs, claws, eyes, spell orbs and moving rune bands a world-space radial discard: an enemy surface remains until the visible holy wave reaches it. Invented glyphs use a local generated DataTexture; no remote assets or accounts. Palaces appear at fixed transforms only after their corresponding ancient clears, with depth prepasses and holy gathering particles; all are solid during the close hold before pullback. `world-swarm.mjs` instances 48 horned flying fiends with articulated bat wings and eyes; light mode uses 24 evenly distributed directions. Their routes cover 360 degrees, stop outside the altar, and send the front rank high above the near camera corridor. Both minions and large enemies use the same radial clearing field. The existing 32-second visible-scene siege deadline and physical-contact altar guard remain; the dragon reaches the front in time, and the demon stays beside the gate. processionalStep clears every beacon foundation for both ruined steps and restored paving. Replay restores all clocks, enemies, islands and beacons. Keep the beam UV clamp, fixed manifestation transforms, original PBR/postprocessing, biography and continuous gallery transitions intact.

Run `node --test tests/*.test.mjs` for discovery, camera/raycast geometry, mapped-surface UVs, biography, and existing entry-route checks.
